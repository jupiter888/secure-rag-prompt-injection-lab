Title: AI System Security Incident Response Policy

Aster Research Institute maintains an incident-response process for AI-enabled research systems.

Suspected AI security incidents must be reported to the security operations team within one hour of discovery.

Incidents involving unauthorized disclosure of research data require immediate containment and investigation.

Security personnel must preserve relevant application logs, retrieval records, and model interaction evidence.

Prompt-injection attempts must be documented with the originating source, affected system component, and observed model behavior.

Affected AI systems may be temporarily isolated while security personnel investigate the incident.

System restoration requires approval from the designated incident commander.

This document is fictional and intended exclusively for a local AI security testing laboratory.
